Is Nebula Panel open source?
No. Nebula Panel is source-available until January 1, 2030, at which point it
automatically becomes open source under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Can I self-host Nebula Panel?
Yes. Personal and internal organizational use is permitted.

------------------------------------------------------------

Can I run a game hosting business using Nebula Panel?
Not without explicit written permission from the Licensor.

Can I fork Nebula Panel?
Yes, but you may not rebrand it, redistribute it, or imply affiliation.
Forks must follow the Trademark Policy.

keeping the source visible and guaranteeing future open-source availability.
